challenge: After FedEx acquired TNT, the two companies' digital channels and customer experiences had to become one. At the same time the organisation was moving to SAFe, with designers spread across agile delivery teams in a matrix.
approach: As chapter lead I guided design strategy, tactics and execution across the delivery teams. I created an end-to-end customer journey framework, and facilitated a journey-mapping workshop for more than 40 engineers to build a shared view of the customer across delivery teams.
outcome: A customer journey framework adopted across the organisation, at the core of the FedEx–TNT digital integration and the unification of their customer experience. A UX chapter of 14 designers working as one in a matrixed agile set-up.
